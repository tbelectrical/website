TB ELECTRICAL — WHERE TO PUT YOUR PHOTOS
=========================================

Drop your files into these folders, then tell Claude you're done.
Don't worry about sizes, cropping or file names — that gets sorted automatically.
Straight off your phone is fine. Bigger is better than smaller.


assets/photos/hero/
-------------------
ONE photo. You and/or your van, outdoors, daylight.
This is the big one at the top of the page.


assets/photos/portfolio/
------------------------
SIX photos of real jobs. One per job.
Good candidates:
  - consumer unit replacement (before AND after if you have both)
  - EV charger install
  - kitchen / downlight job
  - outdoor or garden lighting
  - any commercial work
  - EICR / remedial work

If you have before+after pairs, name them like:
  consumer-unit-before.jpg
  consumer-unit-after.jpg


assets/accreditations/
-------------
Official logo files (PNG with transparent background if possible):
  - NAPIT       — from your NAPIT member portal
  - TrustMark   — trustmark.org.uk, business resources / logo area
  - Rhino Trade Insurance (optional)


ALSO USEFUL
-----------
Anything else you've got — team shots, van livery, tools laid out,
work in progress. Extra photos are never wasted; they can go in
the social share image or future pages.


WHAT CLAUDE NEEDS FROM YOU IN WRITING
-------------------------------------
For each of the six portfolio photos, one line describing the job:
what it was, where (town is enough — no addresses), anything notable.

Example:
  "Full rewire, 3-bed semi in Hitchin. Consumer unit upgrade at the
   same time. Four days, family stayed in the house throughout."

The descriptions currently on the site are placeholders I made up —
they need replacing with your actual jobs.
